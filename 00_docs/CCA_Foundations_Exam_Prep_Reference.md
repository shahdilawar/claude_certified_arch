## Suggested study order (lightweight)

If you're starting from scratch, a reasonable order through this material:

1. **Register first** via the access-request link (Section 1) — registration can take time.
2. **Read 1–2 community guides** (Section 2 blogs) to understand exam scope and format.
3. **Work through the 4 Skilljar learning modules** (Section 3) — these mirror the exam page recommendations.
4. **Study the official Anthropic docs** (Section 4) — especially Hooks, Memory, MCP, and CLI reference.
5. **Watch the recorded prep sessions** (Section 6) — start with "How to prepare…" and "How to Pass…".
6. **Drill the practice exam** (Section 1) until consistently passing.
